Questions we hear most

What do you treat?

Laser hair removal, laser skin rejuvenation, acne, pigmentation, scars and stretch marks, anti-aging therapy, glass glow, Hydrafacial, and 10X treatments for hair, brows and lashes.

What happens at the first consultation?

The dermcosmetologist looks at your skin or hair, asks about your concern, and explains what suits you. You decide whether to go ahead.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the treatment and on you. You get a plan at your consultation.

Is laser hair removal suitable for my skin tone?

Your dermcosmetologist checks your skin and hair type at the consultation and tells you what is suitable.

Can I ask about sensitive areas?

Yes. Our team is discreet and professional, and has heard every question. Ask on WhatsApp if you prefer to start privately.
