# Swathi & Arun invitation V2

Click Play invitation to watch the 39-second film with sound. The film advances at normal speed, with pause/resume, sound, and seeking controls. The page stays in one viewport while the film plays. When it ends, the wedding card becomes available below the film for normal scrolling. Play again restarts the film and hides the card until playback finishes.

Auto framing uses the wide film on screens at least 800 px wide, and the AI-upscaled portrait film on narrower screens. Clarity and Immersive can also be selected manually. Both use `object-fit: contain` so the entire frame stays visible, including on desktop.

This version shares media with `../swathi-arun-immersive-hd/assets/` and adds no duplicate MP4 files. It must be served alongside that folder.
