Questions couples ask

How much do your wedding packages cost?

Premium is ₹1,39,999 and Standard is ₹45,000. Both cover two sessions, the wedding and the reception. *Terms & Conditions apply.

What's the difference between Premium and Standard?

Standard covers traditional photo and video. Premium adds candid photo, candid video, drone coverage, a pre or post wedding photo shoot, a 5-minute cinematic candid video and a second laminated frame.

How many photos and albums do we get?

Unlimited photos and two albums in both packages, plus the Full HD edited video on a pen drive.

Is drone coverage included in Standard?

No. Drone coverage is part of the Premium package.

Which areas do you cover?

Chennai, Chengalpattu, Kanchipuram and Tiruvallur. The studio is in Pallavaram.

Do you shoot other functions?

Yes. Families also book us for engagements, puberty functions, baby showers, maternity and newborn shoots. Message us for what's included.
