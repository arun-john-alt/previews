Questions

Good to know

How much does a setup cost?

Every setup is planned for the event, so we quote once we know the date, the venue and what you'd like. Send those on WhatsApp and we'll come back with a quote.

Can you add live performers?

Yes. Live violin, chendamellam, thappu and dance teams, on their own or together with the DJ.

Which effects do you have?

Cold pyro, fog, CO2, and smoke and bubble effects, often used for the couple's entry.
